# Security

Report vulnerabilities privately to savagetism@icloud.com — do not open
public issues for exploitable weaknesses.

Scope: chain hash verification, content-commitment correctness, proof signature verification, head-pinning and entry-count binding, KnowledgeFound refusal logic.

Out of scope: absence from a mind vs absence from a chain — tabula certifies history only — and the completeness of the scanned chain itself (see THREAT_MODEL.md).
